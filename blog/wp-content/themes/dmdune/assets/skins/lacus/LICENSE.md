# b2 Weblog, Lacus look (May – November 2005): skin assets

- `banner-lacus.webp`: the 430 × 125 banner (`images/lacus.jpg`) from DarkMirage's Weblog, recovered
  from the site's own `images/` folder. The character (Gundam SEED Destiny) belongs to her
  respective owners; DarkMirage's banner art: all rights reserved, not covered by the theme's GPL.

The layout follows the 2005 `layout2b.css` (archived 2005-09-17). The menu bars and date tabs are
shared with the Aegis skin. The tagboard box lists the last entries because the shouts were never
archived. The "advertisements" slot held Google AdSense text ads in 2005; the skin shows a joke
recreation of their look instead: not ads, no links, no advertisers, and a reserved `.example`
domain.
