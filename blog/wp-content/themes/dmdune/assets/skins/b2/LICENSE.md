# b2 Weblog (2003–2005) skin assets

The GAT-X303 Aegis banners (both 2004 versions) and the blue menu and date-tab bars are
from DarkMirage's Weblog, the b2 (cafelog) blog that ran at darkmirage.com/blog/ before WordPress:
all rights reserved by DarkMirage, not covered by the theme's GPL. The originals were recovered from
the site's own `images/` folder; the Nadesico line drawing behind the page from the Wayback Machine.
The mecha shown (Aegis Gundam from Gundam SEED; the Nadesico) belong to their respective owners.
The layout follows the 2004 `layout2b.css`, itself built from Blue Robot's "Layout Reservoir" two
column layout.
