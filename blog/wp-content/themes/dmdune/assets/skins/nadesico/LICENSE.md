# b2 Weblog, Nadesico look (July 2003 – April 2004): skin assets

- `nadesico.webp`: the pale ND-001 Nadesico line drawing that sat behind the posts
  (`blog/nadesico.gif`, recovered from the Wayback Machine). The Nadesico belongs to its
  respective owners.
- `ruri.gif`: the chibi Ruri (in a cat suit) from the midi player box, recovered from the site's own
  `images/` folder.
- `banner-nadesico.webp`: **a stand-in, not the original.** The era's banner (`images/title.jpg`)
  was overwritten by the Aegis art on 2004-04-17 and no copy survives. This one was made in 2026
  from the Nadesico drawing, in the style of the later Aegis banner, so the page has a banner of
  the right size (600 × 130) and period.

DarkMirage's own art: all rights reserved, not covered by the theme's GPL. The layout follows the
2003–04 `layout2b.css` (last archived 2004-04-12), built from Blue Robot's "Layout Reservoir" two
column layout. The menu bars and date tabs (`ctop.gif`, `top.gif`) are shared with the Aegis skin.
